    Settings

    Test data is exchanged with Cloudflare's global network (speed.cloudflare.com), so the server is usually within a few hundred miles of you. Nothing about your test is uploaded anywhere except the random test bytes themselves.

    Understanding your results

    How does the live monitor work?

    It runs a quick 6-second test (ping, download, upload) at the interval you choose while the tab is open, keeps the results on your device, and summarises uptime, drops and the speed range for the last 24 hours. Nothing is sent to a server.

    What counts as a drop?

    A check that fails entirely, or whose download speed is below half of your typical speed for the day. Both show in the table so you can see when they happened.

    Does it work when the laptop is asleep or the tab is in the background?

    Browsers pause background tabs, so keep it in a visible window (a second monitor or a spare phone on the charger works well). Checks skip while the tab is hidden rather than recording false drops.

    Can I use this as evidence with my provider?

    Yes — Copy as CSV exports every check with timestamps. A day of logged evening slowdowns or repeated drops is far more persuasive than one bad speed test.

    How much data does it use?

    Roughly 20–60 MB per check on a fast connection, so about 300 MB–1 GB per hour at 5-minute intervals. Use a longer interval on metered connections.
