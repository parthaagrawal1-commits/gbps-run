About gbps.run

gbps.run is a free, ad-free internet speed test built to answer the question people actually have: is my internet good enough for what I do?

It measures download, upload, ping, jitter and packet loss, and — unlike most tests — ping under load, which is graded as bufferbloat. It then gives plain-English verdicts for 4K streaming, video calls, gaming, working from home and more. It also detects when a VPN or iCloud Private Relay is distorting your results.

  • Runs entirely in your browser; nothing to install.
  • Test data is exchanged with Cloudflare's nearest data centre, which has hundreds of locations worldwide.
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Results are stored only on your own device.
  • Free to embed on any website. Available in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

gbps.run is independent and not affiliated with any internet provider.
